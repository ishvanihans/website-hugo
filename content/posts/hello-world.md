---
title: "goodbye grandma, hello world!"
date: 2026-07-23
comment: true
---

Recently, my grandma passed away. I was very close to her and had been caregiving for her the months leading up to her passing. I expected it to shake me to my core. I thought I'd be spending my days ugly crying over her absence. A loud, intense, in-your-face sorrow. Instead, it's affecting me in the subtlest ways possible. Home without my grandma feels like a toy house and if I stay for too long, the strangeness starts gnawing at me. I thought I'd be all-consumed by her memories but sometimes I forget that she does not exist anymore. One unexpected side effect of her losing her is that I've suddenly developed a sense of urgency. I sat on the idea of shipping my personal website for like ever but suddenly I want to make things? I want to do things? Maybe this is the real legacy my grandma left behind. My bold, vivacious, stubborn grandma. So, hello world! I'm now a part of you. Expect to see more of me.
