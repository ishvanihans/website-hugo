title: "Hello world, goodbye grandma!"
date: 2026-07-23
comment: true
---

Recently, my grandma passed away. I was very close to her and had been caregiving for her the months following her passing. So, naturally I was expecting it to shake me to my core. It didn't. Or at least not in the way I thought it would. I thought I'd be spending my days ugly crying over her absence. Loud. Intense. In-your-face sorrow. Instead, it's affecting me in the weirdest ways possible. The home I grew up in feels like a toyhouse and if I stay for too long, the strangeness starts gnawing at me. I thought I'd be all-consumed by her memories but in actuality, I sometimes forget that she is dead. One weird side effect of her passing I'm noticing is that I've developed a sense of urgency. I sat on the idea of shipping my personal website for like ever but suddenly I want to make things? I want to do things? Death is weird.
