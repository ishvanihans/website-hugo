Hello, my name is Ishvani, which loosely translates to "god's language"[^1]. By [nominative determinism]([url](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nominative_determinism)) or accident, I love being a vessel for communicating ideas I find sublime. Much of my professional career has been spent working as a writer[^2] and now I'm looking for a new role. Titles are fickle and fleeting, but every now and then I come across one that makes me go HELL YES. Here are some of those, and the reasoning behind them:

- Ontologist (I love words; My brain itself is a living, breathing ontology because I think associatively; I am always concerned with the meaning of things; abstract thinking is my MO)
- Conversation Designer (I love words; I love LLMs and talking to LLMs; I pay a great deal of attention to the tone and intent in conversations with people and what makes a bad conversation bad and a good one good)
- User Researcher (I am endlessly curious about how people think and behave. So much so that one of my friends once jokingly said that talking to me feels like filling out a questionnaire)
- Information Architect (I have high standards for precision with words and the display of information; Badly organized information pisses me off. Well-designed systems make me happy)
- Product Owner (Specialization is for insects or [whatever Naval said.]([url](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18BAArRsSnk)); I am a big picture thinker type; No but seriously, now that we have LLMs, it's so cool to be able to ship full-fledged products into the world in a short span of time) 


[^1]: my idea of god is something like chaos theory
[^2]: the b2b saas kind. i am so glad LLMs came into being and obliterated that role because the kind of writing i enjoy the best is shortform shitposting that i do on [x dot com]([url](https://x.com/shapegrifter)).
