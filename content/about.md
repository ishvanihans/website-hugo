Hello, my name is Ishvani, which loosely translates to "god's language"[^1]. By [nominative determinism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nominative_determinism) or accident, I love being a vessel for communicating ideas I find sublime. Much of my professional career has been spent working as a writer[^2] and now I'm looking for a new role. Titles are fickle and fleeting, but every now and then I come across one that makes me go HELL YES. Here are some of those, and why:

- Ontologist (I love words; My brain itself is a living, breathing ontology because I think associatively; I am always concerned with the meaning of things; abstract thinking is my MO)
- Conversation Designer (I love words; I love LLMs and talking to LLMs; I pay a great deal of attention to the tone and intent in conversations with people and what makes a bad conversation bad and a good one good)
- User Researcher (I am endlessly curious about how people think and behave. So much so that one of my friends once jokingly said that talking to me feels like filling out a questionnaire[^3])
- Information Architect (I have high standards for precision with words; Badly organized information pisses me off; Well-designed systems make me happy)
- Product Owner (Specialization is for insects or [whatever Naval said](https://youtube.com/shorts/XwOnl1m60LA?si=StxETRKpoeEuVdUO). No but seriously, now that we have LLMs, it's so fun to be able ship full-fledged products where you're the sole designer, developer, writer, thinker, etc. in a short span of time; if you care about an idea, why wouldn't you want to take complete ownership of it?)
- Venture Capitalist (I need capital and experience in the markets to get here but I love scouting and betting on dark horses, and predicting which ideas have mana)
